# Interview Script

## 30-Second Explanation

I built an Aviation Cyber Risk Mapping project that connects aviation maintenance and operational systems to cybersecurity risks. It maps systems like maintenance records, parts inventory, scheduling, GPS awareness, and ground networks to threats, impacts, and controls.

## Strong Interview Answer

My background is in aviation maintenance, so I wanted to show how that experience transfers into cybersecurity. Aviation relies heavily on trusted records, traceable parts, accurate documentation, and secure support systems. This project identifies how cyber failures in those areas could affect readiness, safety, and operations.

## Resume Bullet

Created an Aviation Cyber Risk Mapping project connecting maintenance records, parts traceability, scheduling, navigation awareness, and ground networks to cybersecurity threats, operational impacts, and recommended controls.
