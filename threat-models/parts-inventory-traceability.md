# Threat Model — Parts Inventory and Traceability

## Asset

Parts records, inventory status, serial numbers, shelf-life data, and vendor documentation.

## Threat

Incorrect, missing, or manipulated parts data.

## Why It Matters

Aviation maintenance depends on knowing whether parts are approved, traceable, available, and serviceable.

## Risk Examples

- Part marked serviceable when it is not
- Shelf-life data missing or incorrect
- Serial number mismatch
- Inventory system unavailable during maintenance
- Vendor document phishing or spoofing

## Impact

- Delayed maintenance
- Readiness loss
- Compliance issue
- Safety risk if incorrect part is used

## Controls

- Role-based inventory changes
- Serial number validation
- Change history
- Vendor verification process
- Backups
- Periodic inventory reconciliation
