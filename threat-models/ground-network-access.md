# Threat Model — Ground Network Access

## Asset

Maintenance office networks, admin systems, Wi-Fi, shared devices, and support systems.

## Threat

Unauthorized access through weak credentials, phishing, exposed services, or poor segmentation.

## Why It Matters

Ground systems support maintenance, scheduling, inventory, documentation, and communications.

## Failure Paths

- Shared passwords
- No MFA
- Personal devices on sensitive networks
- Flat network architecture
- Old systems without patches
- Weak logging

## Impact

- Credential theft
- Data exposure
- Operational disruption
- Unauthorized changes to support systems

## Controls

- MFA
- Network segmentation
- Strong password policy
- Device inventory
- Patch management
- Logging and monitoring
